# Scoping & Documentation Assessor & Feedback Skill

Use this skill to critically evaluate the **entire documentation corpus** of a project against the **7-Domain Scoping & Documentation Rubric**.

## Rubric Domains & Pass Cutlines

To **PASS**, the project's documentation MUST be at a **Competent** level on **1 or more** sub-criteria in all 7 domains:

1. **Problem Definition** (*>= 1 Competent*): Clear business problem, technical opportunity, customer success unlocks, CUJs, and Definition of Done.
2. **Technical Scope & Constraints** (*>= 1 Competent*): Precise technical scope, constraints/assumptions, system boundaries, pre-build architectural approach, and prototype vs. production separation.
3. **Stakeholder Alignment & Success Criteria** (*>= 1 Competent*): Stakeholder alignment, measurable success/acceptance criteria, and phased delivery plan / architectural roadmap.
4. **System Design Artifacts** (*>= 1 Competent*): Accurate architecture, data flow, and sequence diagrams facilitating peer review and alignment.
5. **Decision Records** (*>= 1 Competent*): Comprehensive ADRs documenting trade-offs, alternatives considered, rationale, and historical context.
6. **API Documentation** (*>= 1 Competent*): OpenAPI / tool contract specs, integration guides, SDK docs, and predictable usage examples.
7. **Operational Documentation** (*>= 1 Competent*): Deployment guides, runbooks, onboarding docs, troubleshooting guides, and handoff continuity.
