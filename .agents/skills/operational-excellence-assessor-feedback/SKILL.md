# Operational Excellence Assessor & Feedback Skill

Use this skill to critically evaluate any project, specification, or component against the **4-Domain Operational Excellence Rubric**.

## Rubric Domains & Pass Cutlines

To **PASS**, the target MUST be at a **Competent** level on **1 or more** sub-criteria in every domain:

1. **CI/CD & Deployment** (*>= 1 Competent*):
   - `1.1` Automated pipelines with blue/green or canary deployment strategies.
   - `1.2` Rollback automation ensuring safe, repeatable code releases.
2. **Infrastructure as Code** (*>= 1 Competent*):
   - `2.1` Terraform/Pulumi and Cloud Build for reproducible environments.
   - `2.2` Strict environment parity between development, staging, and production.
3. **AI Lifecycle Management** (*>= 1 Competent*):
   - `3.1` Model/agent versioning, A/B testing, and configuration.
   - `3.2` Experiment tracking and operational tooling for scalable agent management.
4. **Testing & Quality Engineering** (*>= 1 Competent*):
   - `4.1` Unit, integration, and e2e testing with load/performance validation.
   - `4.2` Maximized test automation and coverage ensuring production-grade reliability.
